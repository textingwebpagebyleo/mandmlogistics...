WHO WE ARE

Your Partner in Sourcing, Procurement and International Logistics

M&M Logistics (SL) Ltd is a sourcing, procurement and logistics company focused on helping individuals and businesses access products from China and coordinate their movement to West African markets.

We recognise that purchasing products internationally can involve challenges such as identifying suitable suppliers, communicating with manufacturers, coordinating purchases, arranging transportation and following up on shipments.

Our role is to make these processes more organised by supporting customers from their initial product enquiry through procurement and shipping coordination.

  • Product sourcing and supplier identification
  • Purchasing and procurement coordination
  • Warehouse and shipment preparation coordination
  • Sea freight, air freight and courier arrangements
  • Shipment updates and customer support

OUR REGIONAL REACH

From China to West Africa

Our sourcing operations connect customers with products and suppliers in China, with shipping destinations across selected West African markets.

Connecting Markets. Moving Businesses Forward.

China is an important sourcing origin for our procurement and logistics activities. We support customers seeking products from Chinese suppliers and coordinate the associated purchasing and shipping arrangements.

Our current destination focus includes Sierra Leone, Ghana and Nigeria, supporting individuals, traders and businesses with different sourcing requirements.

Depending on the nature of the shipment, available arrangements may include sea freight, air freight and courier services.
