WHO WE ARE

Your Partner in Sourcing, Procurement and International Logistics

M&M Logistics (SL) Ltd is a sourcing, procurement and logistics company focused on helping individuals and businesses access products from China and coordinate their movement to West African markets.

CLEAR AND TRANSPARENT PRICING

Understanding Our Product Sourcing Charges

For product sourcing and procurement orders, the M&M Logistics service fee is calculated using the total product purchase value. A flat processing fee also applies.

Upfront Processing Fee $50

A flat processing fee payable for each customer procurement order, in addition to the applicable percentage service fee.

PURCHASE VALUE 10%

Standard Procurement Tier

Applies to qualifying product purchases from $1,500 up to below $10,000.

$1,500 – Below $10,000
PURCHASE VALUE 5%

Mid-Value Procurement Tier

Applies to product purchases of $10,000 up to and including $50,000.

$10,000 – $50,000
PURCHASE VALUE 3%

High-Value Procurement Tier

Applies to product purchases above $50,000.

Above $50,000
Important information about procurement charges
  • The $50 upfront processing fee applies to each customer procurement order and is additional to the percentage service fee.
  • The percentage is determined by the total product purchase value and the applicable tier.
  • Purchases below $1,500 are not covered by the percentage schedule. Contact us for clarification before proceeding.
  • The procurement service fee is separate from the supplier's product price and shipping costs.

Example: How the charges are calculated

If the product purchase value is $8,000:

Product purchase value: $8,000

Procurement service fee at 10%: $800

Upfront processing fee: $50

M&M Logistics fees in this example: $850

The product purchase price remains $8,000. Shipping, customs, taxes and other applicable third-party charges are not included in the $850 service-fee calculation.
