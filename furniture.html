Know Your Buying Costs Before You Proceed

Every customer pays a one-time flat upfront processing fee, plus a service percentage based on the total value of the furniture or products purchased.

$50

PURCHASE VALUE

Please note: The $50 upfront processing fee is additional to the applicable percentage service charge. The percentage is calculated on the total value of the furniture purchase. Shipping, customs duties, taxes, inspection and other third-party charges are separate unless expressly included in your written quotation. Purchases below $1,500 are not covered by the percentage schedule shown above; please contact our team for clarification.
