OUR PROCESS AND PRICING

How Our Services Work

Each of our three service divisions follows a different process and pricing structure. Explore how we work and what charges apply.

DIVISION 01

Logistics & Procurement

From product sourcing and procurement to international air and sea freight.

  1. 1Customer submits product requirements and specifications.
  2. 2M&M identifies suitable products and suppliers.
  3. 3Customer reviews and approves the quotation.
  4. 4Procurement and purchasing coordination begins.
  5. 5Goods are received and prepared for shipment.
  6. 6Air or sea freight is arranged and shipment updates are provided.

Procurement Service Charges

Minimum order value $1,500
Upfront processing fee $50
$1,500 – $10,000 10%
Above $10,000 – $50,000 5%
Above $50,000 3%

Orders below $1,500 are not accepted. The $50 upfront fee applies to every accepted order in addition to the applicable percentage service charge. Percentages are calculated on the full purchase value. Product costs, freight, customs duties and other applicable charges are separate.

DIVISION 02

China Visa Assistance

Application assistance for individuals travelling to China for business or tourism.

  1. 1Customer selects Business Visa or Tourist Visa assistance.
  2. 2M&M provides the relevant document requirements.
  3. 3Customer submits the required application documents.
  4. 4Application preparation and assistance are provided.
  5. 5Customer follows the relevant submission and appointment process.
  6. 6The application outcome is communicated by the relevant authorities.

Visa Assistance Charges

Initial assistance fee $50
Additional fee upon approval $50
Total M&M fees upon approval $100

These charges cover M&M's visa application assistance only. They do not include the official Chinese Embassy visa application fee or other applicable third-party charges. Visa approval is not guaranteed and remains subject to the decision of the relevant authorities.

DIVISION 03

China Business & Travel Support

Practical travel arrangements and market guidance for visitors to China.

  1. 1Customer submits travel dates and visit requirements.
  2. 2M&M discusses the support services required.
  3. 3Airport pickup and transportation arrangements are discussed.
  4. 4Suitable hotel accommodation is identified and arranged.
  5. 5Market guidance and visit arrangements are planned.
  6. 6Customer receives the agreed travel support.

Travel Support Charges

Initial arrangement fee $50
Airport pickup By quotation
Hotel reservation By quotation
China market guide By quotation

Additional charges depend on the services selected, travel locations, accommodation requirements and duration. A quotation will be provided before arrangements are confirmed.
