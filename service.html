China Visa Assistance

Planning to travel to China? Understanding the application process and preparing the necessary documentation are important first steps.

M&M Logistics provides guidance and administrative assistance to customers preparing for their China visa applications.

  • Initial application guidance
  • Visa document checklist
  • Document preparation support
  • Application information review
  • Appointment guidance
  • Travel preparation guidance

Our Service Fees & Pricing Structure

Understand the stated M&M service fees before scheduling a consultation.

Logistics & Procurement

For product sourcing and procurement orders.

$50

Initial processing fee

  • Minimum order value: $1,500

Initial arrangement fee
