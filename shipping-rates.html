UNDERSTANDING AIR FREIGHT

How Chargeable Weight Works

Air freight is charged using the greater of the actual weight and the volumetric weight.

1. Actual Weight

The actual weight is the physical weight of your shipment measured on a scale.

2. Volumetric Weight

Volumetric weight represents the space occupied by your shipment. A large but lightweight package can have a higher volumetric weight than its physical weight.

Volumetric weight (kg) = Length × Width × Height (cm) ÷ 6,000
This is equivalent to approximately 167 kg per CBM.

3. Chargeable Weight

The greater of the actual weight and volumetric weight is used as the chargeable weight for the estimate.

UNDERSTANDING SEA FREIGHT

How Sea Freight Charges Are Calculated

Sea freight charges are based on the cubic volume occupied by your shipment.

What is CBM?

CBM means cubic metre. It measures the three-dimensional space occupied by your cargo.

CBM = Length (m) × Width (m) × Height (m)

Example calculation

Assume your shipment measures 2 metres long, 1 metre wide and 1 metre high.

Volume = 2 × 1 × 1 = 2 CBM

Estimated freight = 2 × $130 = $260

This is an illustrative calculation using the displayed rate. Final measurements and applicable charges must be confirmed.
